Thanks for trying QQR

If something did not work, one of these fixes may help. QQR did not send any data when it was removed; this page only opened in your browser.

I could not find the scanner

Click the puzzle icon at the top right of Chrome and pin QQR, then click its icon and choose Select area. You can also press Ctrl+Shift+Y (⌘+Shift+Y on a Mac) or right-click an image and choose Scan QR/barcode here.

A code would not scan

  • Include the whole code and a little empty space around it in your selection.
  • Zoom the page in so small codes are larger before selecting them.
  • Paste a screenshot into QQR's Scan tab, or use the webcam scanner in the workspace.
  • Chrome blocks extensions on its own pages, such as the Web Store and settings.

Tell us what went wrong

Feedback decides what gets fixed next. Share what happened in the public support tracker. Please do not include private QR contents, passwords, or account numbers.
